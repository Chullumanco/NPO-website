Executive Summary

Linamandla Kaone Foundation is a non-profit organization established to cater for the needs of the disadvantaged community of Langa and other surrounding communities. As the name suggests, Linamandla Kaone, the foundation of the organization is church based and its pillar is God. The organization provides assistance to schools, churches and individual families with the help of surrounding businesses, community members and other stakeholders.
