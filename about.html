Mission & Vision
statement

To change and improve the attitude and social functioning of individuals, families and the community as a whole through sport and other community development activities that will assist in poverty alleviation and skills development. The organization had established a project specifically aimed at youth to alleviate the problem of alcohol and drug abuse which results in gangsterism.

One of the specific needs that have been identified is in the area of Sports, Arts and Gospel music, there are no sports facilities in the area which makes it hard to attract youth to participate in sport, the organization strongly believes that, sport and arts can make a big change in the life of our youngsters, in this community.

Youth in its nature is full of energy and when that energy cannot be used elsewhere, positively, they tend to use it negatively and end up indulging in drugs and criminal activities to make ends meet, which makes the crime rate sky high.
